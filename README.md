## Welcome to My Lab

This is my personal technology laboratory, a space dedicated to the systems I know well, the technologies I'm passionate about, and the things I'm curious to explore.

Linux, UNIX, networking, and information security have always been more than just work to me. I enjoy understanding how systems operate, experimenting with configurations, investigating problems, and discovering what happens behind the scenes.

From building and securing servers to testing their defenses, this lab is a place to explore both sides of security.

Some experiments revisit familiar territory. Others lead to something completely new. There's always something worth exploring.

☕ **Powered by coffee, curiosity, and a genuine passion for technology.**
